# Al Qubtan Restaurants Radio

The restaurant branches share a centrally managed radio broadcast throughout their operating hours.

## Language

**Broadcast**

The shared sequence of music heard by connected listeners. While it is on air, joining listeners hear the current passage. After the last listener disconnects, playback stops until a listener starts it again.

**Branch**

A restaurant location that listens to the broadcast during its operating hours.

**Station playlist**

The repeating, ordered selection of tracks that central management programs for the broadcast. Branches hear the same selection as it goes on air.

**Playlist entry**

One occurrence of a library track at a particular place in the station playlist. A manager can move or remove each occurrence independently.

**Music library**

The station’s collection of tracks ready for a manager to find by artist or song title and add to the station playlist. A track can be in the library before the manager schedules it to play.
